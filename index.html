The Gray Capture team10

Gray Capture started in 2020, when Kofi began assisting other photographers around Accra.

Since then it's grown into a small team — photographers, an editor, and hands on the ground for setup and lighting — built around the same belief that started it: a photograph should feel considered, not just correct. Based in Accra, working across Ghana.
